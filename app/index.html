Product C · Dennis

Member Support

Members ask about classes, levels, instructors, and policies — and get answers from the studio's real schedule.

  • Answers come from the studio's actual schedule and written policies
  • When it has no answer, it says so plainly instead of inventing one
  • Open all day, every day, right on the studio's site

Product D · Rensley

Member Re-engagement

Staff see which active members used to come and have gone quiet — with the evidence — and get a personal draft to review and send themselves.

  • Flags members quiet for more than 14 days, ranked by who's most worth saving
  • Every flag shows its evidence: last visit, usual class, usual instructor
  • Works on the studio's own attendance export, entirely in the browser
  • Drafts only — a person reads, decides, and sends every message
Open Member Re-engagement

One vocabulary, four lanes

Every product reads the same shared records — members, memberships, class sessions, reservations, attendance — through one data contract. Only a class a member actually attended counts as a visit; a no-show never does. Each product folder belongs to one developer, and their color follows their work everywhere.
