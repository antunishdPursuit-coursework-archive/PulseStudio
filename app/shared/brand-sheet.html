01 — The mark

A heartbeat, drawn once.

A single pulse line. It is the studio's promise in one stroke — that a member is seen, and that something is still moving.

One path, three places, never retyped

M2 16h5.5l3-8.5 5 17 3.5-10 2.5 1.5H30

It lives in app/favicon.svg and as PULSE_MARK_PATH in shared/components/logo.ts. Every other asset — the social card, the touch icon, this page — pulls that same string rather than copying it. A third hand-typed version is how three copies drift apart.

It borrows its colour

The stroke is currentColor. Put the mark in a header and it is the foreground; put it on a fill and it is the ink. It is never given a colour of its own, which is why it works on both grounds and inside all four products without a variant.

Sizes, from tokens

20px
compact
26px
standard
hero
art

Compact sits in a product header beside the wordmark. Standard is the front door's own header. The wide line across the landing hero is an art treatment of the same path — not a third logo. Sizes come from --pulse-mark-compact / --pulse-mark-standard, never a local pixel value.

What not to do

Do not recolour it outside currentColor. Do not add a fill. Do not stretch it — the stroke weight is tuned to the 32-unit box. Do not put a constantly moving mark inside a dense product panel; it belongs in the header, where a person is not reading past it.

02 — Typography

A shout and a speaking voice.

Two faces, chosen so they cannot be mistaken for each other. One exists to be seen across a room; the other exists to be read for a minute without noticing it.

Anton display · 400 · one weight
ABCDEFGHIJKLM
NOPQRSTUVWXYZ
1234567890

Anton ships one weight, and it is already the heavy one. There is no light Anton to fall back to, which is the point: it is used where the studio raises its voice — the wordmark, page titles, the number on a stat — and nowhere else.

Condensed, so a long word like RE-ENGAGEMENT still fits a narrow header without being set smaller than its neighbours. Set it tight, in caps, with a touch of letter-spacing so the heavy strokes do not close up.

Never set body copy in Anton. At paragraph sizes its counters fill in and the line becomes a texture rather than a sentence.

Epilogue text · 400–700 variable
Regular 400 — the reading weight
Semibold 600 — labels and emphasis
Bold 700 — the loudest text gets

One variable file covers 400 to 700, so weight is a continuum rather than three separate downloads. A humanist sans with open apertures and unambiguous figures — which matters on a screen that says 54 days quiet beside a person's name.

It carries everything Anton does not: sentences, evidence lines, instructions, the words of a draft message. If a reader should understand it rather than be struck by it, it is Epilogue.

Why this pairing

Maximum contrast of role with no contrast of era. Both are contemporary grotesques, so they sit together without period clash — but one is condensed and immovable at a single weight, the other open and fluid across four. A reader never has to wonder which is speaking.

Both are self-hosted, and that is a promise

app/shared/fonts/anton-400.woff2 · epilogue-variable.woff2 — served from this origin. No Google Fonts link, no CDN, no third party contacted on any page. Licensed under the SIL Open Font License; both licences ship beside the files. When a page claims a visitor's data never leaves their browser, this is part of why that is true.

White and black are the built-ins. A person may also choose their own background and text pair in Settings; the shared control keeps that pair readable rather than trusting the choice. Backgrounds always come from var(--bg) — never a literal, never a gradient.

An accent is an identity, not automatically legible. Each ratio above is that colour used as TEXT on the light ground, against WCAG AA's 4.5:1. Three sit below it. That is recorded rather than hidden: docs/contrast-baseline.json names each one and its owner, and only that owner clears it — a developer's colour is theirs. Product D added a --accent-strong companion for text and cleared its own. check-contrast.mjs fails any new pairing below AA, measuring with the same module the browser runs.

04 — Motion, assets, voice

Alive, not busy.

Motion

One spark travels the pulse line every 3.6 seconds — the same beat the landing hero keeps — and then the mark rests.

Stroke and opacity only. Never transform, never layout: nothing outside the mark's own box repaints, and one cycle in 3.6s is nowhere near a flashing threshold.

Rest is the default state, not a fallback. The spark is a second copy of the path, invisible until animated, and every moving rule sits inside prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference. Someone who has asked their system for less motion sees a complete, solid mark — not a degraded one.

The assets

favicon.svg — the mark, transparent, stroke in currentColor. Transparent because it composites onto the browser's own chrome.

apple-touch-icon.png — 180×180, and opaque white on purpose. A home-screen icon lands on whatever wallpaper a person chose; a transparent mark with a white stroke would vanish on a light one.

og-image.png — 1200×630 for link previews. Raster, because the platforms that unfurl a link do not reliably render SVG.

Each is generated from the same path string. The SVG sources sit beside the PNGs so the raster is never the only copy.

Voice

The studio speaks to a person, never about the project. Product letters and builder names stay off anything a member reads — authorship is carried by colour, not by a byline.

State negatives explicitly. A screen with nothing to show says what it checked: "5 members checked, 0 flagged" — never a blank panel.

Never claim what was not measured. If a record is silent, the copy says so rather than filling the gap with something plausible. A draft that guessed once produced "your last class class" in a note about to reach a real member.
