Privacy policy

Short version: this website keeps what you do in your own browser, sends nothing to anybody else, and has no way to follow you around the internet. The long version says exactly what that means, so you can check it rather than take our word for it.

Last updated 23 August 2026.

What we store, and where

Everything below is kept by your own browser, on your own device, under this website's address. None of it is sent to us or to anybody else, and clearing your browser's site data for this address erases all of it.

  • Who you are signed in as. Picking your name from the top bar records that choice so the pages can greet you and show your own bookings. There is no password, and it is convenience rather than protection — see the note about that below.
  • Your class reservations. Booking a spot records it here so the schedule and the studio's other screens agree with each other.
  • How you want the site to look. Light, dark, or a background and text pair you chose yourself on the settings page.
  • Staff working notes. On the staff tools only: which members have already been contacted, and who has asked not to be. Those stay in the staff member's own browser.

What we do not do

Stated as a list rather than a promise, because each one is something you can verify in a browser's network panel:

  • No cookies. Not one, of any kind.
  • No analytics and no tag manager. Nothing counts your visits, records your clicks, or builds a profile.
  • No third-party hosts. No advertising network, no embedded player, no social widget. Both typefaces this site uses are served from this address rather than borrowed from a font service, which is otherwise one of the quietest ways a page tells somebody else you were here.
  • No selling or sharing. There is nothing to sell: we do not receive anything to sell.

The one thing that leaves your browser

If you type a question into the support assistant, that question is sent to the studio's own assistant service so it can be answered. Nothing else goes with it — not your name, not your bookings, not an identifier. If you would rather ask a person, the contact details at the bottom of every page reach the front desk directly.

Signing in is convenience, not a lock

Signing in on this site picks your name from a list. It has no password, it is remembered only by your own browser, and it does not protect anything. We are telling you this rather than letting it look like security: do not treat any page here as private because you signed in. Every page on this site is reachable by anyone with its address.

The people in these records

The members, instructors and classes shown on this site are invented. They are generated from a fixed starting number, which is why the same studio appears on every device. No real member's record is published here.

Erasing everything

Clear this site's data in your browser's settings and every item in the first list above is gone. There is no account to delete, because there is no account on our side.

Questions

Write to us at the address in the footer of this page, or ask the front desk. A person reads it.
