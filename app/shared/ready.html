The five-minute walkthrough

  1. Open the front door. It speaks to a member — book, ask, and "never go unnoticed" — and the pulse line beats.
  2. Sign in from the top bar as a member: the front door greets them by name and takes them straight to their classes; the same persona follows every page.
  3. Book a class Booking — the week day by day, spots left, and a confirmation naming the class, time, and instructor. Your reservations appear under the schedule with a cancel on each.
  4. Open the Dashboard Dashboard — the week's fill rates and the classes needing attention. Say the gap out loud here: this board still builds its own studio, so the booking from step 3 is not on it yet — one config line away, and the honest thing to show.
  5. Ask the assistant Support a schedule question — answered from today's real schedule, refusals stated.
  6. Sign in as Front Desk and open Re-engagement Re-engage — the quiet ranked with evidence; a member who booked back in is named and left alone; copy the draft.
  7. Close on the story: storytold — the five beats and the map where every finished hand-off literally fires.
